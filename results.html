Cake Scan Showdown · final results

Two pizzas, a cake, a glass of juice and a cocktail

The final capture: one medium pizza (about 30 cm), scanned twice as a Gaussian splat, first plain and then with a ketchup drizzle, with the same setup and camera settings, so the two can be compared side by side, plus a glass of juice, the hardest test because of the clear glass, polished in Blender, a cocktail topped with cream and cashews, and a veggie pizza and a layered mousse cake shot with a full-frame Sony camera instead of a phone.     Capture recipe that worked best

    Background

    A plain, matte white wall and a white mat with random bold pen marks that turns with the food. Green screens left green reflections on plates and glass that had to be cleaned out afterwards.

    Light and camera

    • One large diffused light above the turntable, no ring light
    • Pro mode, 1× lens, ISO 100, 1/100 s, fixed white balance and focus
    • The same exposure for every ring

    Photos

    • Three rings of 60 photos (6° per turn) at low, middle and high angles
    • Tethered to the PC, so the phone is never touched
    • Check every ring lines up before training
