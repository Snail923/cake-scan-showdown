The best scan of every dish

After comparing three phone apps, Meshroom, Agisoft Metashape and Gaussian splatting on the same captures, the Gaussian splats looked the most real: they keep glass, gloss and fine crumbs that a textured mesh loses. These are the final, cleaned splats. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap View on your table on a phone to place the dish in AR (iPhone and Android).

    Capture recipe that worked best

    Background

    A plain, matte white wall and a white mat with random bold pen marks that turns with the food. Green screens left green reflections on plates and glass that had to be cleaned out afterwards.

    Light and camera

    • One large diffused light above the turntable, no ring light
    • Pro mode, 1× lens, ISO 100, 1/100 s, fixed white balance and focus
    • The same exposure for every ring

    Photos

    • Three rings of 60 photos (6° per turn) at low, middle and high angles
    • Tethered to the PC, so the phone is never touched
    • Check every ring lines up before training
